# Custom MIDI Design Solutions

Electrovoce specializes in custom MIDI device design. We also offer contract engineering services for any music technology design needs.

If you want a MIDI device that you can't find in the marketplace, we can create it for you. We have over 7 years experience helping customers bring their ideas to life. Whether you're creating a product for market or creating a new cyber-art work, we can provide a design solution.

Our latest customers include the Experience Music Project Museum in Seattle, WA. We designed the clocks and audio relays in the Sound Lab, and created a MIDI routing solution for the Jambo drum. We've helped design products for handicaped kids, and have contributed to numerous musical art projects around the world.
