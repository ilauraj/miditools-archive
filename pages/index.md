# Electrovoce

We provide custom MIDI design solutions and distribute the MIDItools® product line.

If you're into electronic music technology, and want to learn more about how it works, you've come to the right place. MIDItools® products were designed to educate and to enable artistic technologists to achieve their goals.

Design solutions based on MIDItools® reside around the world, from the Experience Music Project Museum to numerous personal studios. Coupled with the book Digital Projects for Musicians, by Craig Anderton, Bob Moses and Greg Bartlett, MIDItools has helped teach classroom students electronics principles and MIDI.

Please enjoy exploring our site.
